# Data attribution

The generated `cities.json` and `countries.json` datasets contain a curated derivative of the
[GeoNames geographical database](https://www.geonames.org/), specifically the
`cities500` export distributed from the
[GeoNames download server](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/).

GeoNames data is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The dataset has been filtered, enriched with country and continent metadata,
and spatially sampled for use by Flight Map Wallpaper. GeoNames is not
responsible for these changes.

Generation counts and the source dataset name are recorded in
`cities.metadata.json`.
